Methodology, Data Lags & Limitations

Observed vs. Baseline Data: Macro indicators fetched from the World Bank Open API reflect the latest verified national reporting year (typically a 1–2 year statistical lag), while Open-Meteo air quality sensors report near real-time hourly readings. Composite goal scores (79.0 overall; 69.7 spillover) use UN SDSN / ABS baselines.

Simulation & Modelling: The Post-2030 Transformation Lab, Doughnut overshoot wedges, and 2026–2050 trajectories are interactive exploratory scenario models designed to illustrate system synergies, trade-offs, and policy sequencing—not econometric forecasts.
